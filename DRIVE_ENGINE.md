**# Drive Engine Philosophy**



**The goal of the MiniSkidi drive engine is not to emulate an RC tank.**



**The goal is to emulate the feel of operating a real skid steer loader using a modern game controller.**



**Every steering change shall be evaluated against one question:**



**"Does this make the MiniSkidi feel more like a real skid steer?"**



**If the answer is no, the change does not belong in the project.**
